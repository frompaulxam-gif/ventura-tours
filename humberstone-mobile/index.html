BEFORE YOU GET IN TOUCH

A few useful questions.

Is my model supported?

Ask the shop about your exact make, model and issue. The illustrative phones and symptom choices above do not establish repair coverage or stock.

Can I get a price here?

No price is calculated on this page. Ask the shop to confirm any assessment fee, parts options, total cost and terms before you agree to work.

What about buying or selling a phone?

Call to ask about the device, condition and any current requirements. No stock, offer or valuation is promised here.

Will these notes book a repair?

No. They stay on this page until you clear or leave it. Copy them for your own use and contact the shop separately. Never share your passcode or account password in these notes.
