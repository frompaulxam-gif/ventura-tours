# Two distinct AI transformations

Separate comparison previews, retaining Signal Studio and the production sites.

- `?variation=bottleneck`: a physical manual gate blocks fifteen illustrated work packets. Two shields retract to expose an AI router. The packets pass through the gate into three labelled output trays. The backlog disappears. Final composition is centred over the headline.
- `?variation=dashboard`: detached charts and source records assemble into a single dimensional business dashboard. A summary sits at the bottom for human review. The completed overview moves beside the headline on desktop, and above it on mobile.

Each uses different original Blender geometry and motion. Both run on a short 230svh track. Switching starts the selected transformation from the beginning. No autoplay, artificial scroll locking, gradient backgrounds or video playback.

The actual Blender-exported `assets/ai-directions.glb` contains 15 named asset groups. The editable source, original modelling script and render live at `outputs/blender-directions/` in the workspace. Geometry parts are merged by material; meshes and paths are created once. Rendering stops when settled or offscreen. Scrolling is reversible; reduced motion and short windows use the final composition with ordinary scrolling. WebGL failure falls back to the headline and comparison content.

Existing local Three.js r180, GLTFLoader and material environment are reused under their existing MIT licence. No new runtime dependencies. Content and charts are illustrative concepts, not live business records.
