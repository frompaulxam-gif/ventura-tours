What is Nutrients93?+

Nutrients93 Ltd is an independent Saladmaster dealer. We help people explore Saladmaster cookware through cooking demonstrations and personal guidance.

How do I buy the cookware?+

Start with a conversation or cooking demonstration. The Nutrients93 team can explain the available pieces, current pricing and ordering options.

Do I need to be an experienced cook?+

No. A demonstration is a chance to learn, whether you cook every day or are just getting started. Bring your questions and your appetite.

Can we talk about dietary preferences?+

Yes. Tell the team about dietary preferences and any allergies before arranging a demonstration so they can discuss what is suitable.
