BMF — 11 tested recreation prompts

Open https://venturasolutions.co.uk/bmfposters/prompts.html to choose a poster, edit the information and copy the complete prompt. Upload one ordinary person or gym photograph to image generation. Do not upload the selected poster, Pinterest format, logo or brand palette image. The entire poster, including text, is generated.

The prompts describe type shapes, line breaks, colours, photography, effects and positions. Each final prompt was actually tested using one raw photo only; 20 image generation calls were made across 11 formats. Test samples and individual findings are provided. These recreate the format; they cannot guarantee exact pixels, fonts or photo angles. 04-performance remains a partial layout match because its title drifts upwards. BMF marks described in text are approximations.

Timetables were checked against all eight time slots in Monday, Wednesday and Friday, plus Tuesday/Thursday open sessions and Saturday HYROX. Never Feel Lost copy and event wording were visually checked. The retained event date/time are historical sample content; replace them with the actual event information before posting. Both timetable matrices were reproduced correctly in the final tests.

Built-in image generation was used for the complete posters and lettering. The tool does not expose its model version, so GPT Image 2.5 cannot be verified. Local scripts package files, lossless previews and the website; they do not render the poster text.

Files: prompts/ contains one standalone text template plus the exact tested prompt per poster. media/ contains the final photo-only generation test for each. test-report.json records output dimensions, hashes, method and observed differences. The selected artwork is kept unchanged on the QC page and in BMF-nice-ones-11.zip, except the requested readability revision to 01-lost.
