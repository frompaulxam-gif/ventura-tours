What was tested

All 11 final prompts were actually generated with one raw photograph as the only image input. No Pinterest, finished poster, palette or logo image was supplied. The complete poster and its text were generated together. There were 20 generation calls, including revisions.

The samples show each actual result. Font shapes, placement, crop and generated face angle can vary. The BMF mark described in text is an approximation. The 04-performance title still drifts upwards after three attempts.
