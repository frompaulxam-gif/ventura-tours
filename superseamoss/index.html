BEFORE YOU ORDER

Know your jar.

How do I order?+

Build your mix-and-match order above, then copy your enquiry and send it to Super Seamoss on Instagram. Online checkout is being set up; the team will confirm availability and dispatch.

How much is delivery?+

Chilled next-day delivery to UK mainland is £9.95. Standard orders get free delivery when the jar subtotal is £50 or more, excluding postage. Every subscription includes free chilled delivery, with no minimum spend. Confirm availability and dispatch timing with the team.

Can I subscribe?+

Subscribe & Save gives you 10% off every recurring order, with free chilled UK mainland delivery, cancel anytime, priority dispatch and an occasional subscriber-only flavour. Choose the subscription option in your enquiry. Subscriptions will be billed and dispatched monthly. Subscription checkout is being set up.

What’s inside each blend?+

Open a blend above to see the ingredients featured in its original Instagram post. Ask the team for the full current ingredient and allergen information before ordering.

Are all the blends vegan?+

Manuka Glow contains manuka honey. Check your chosen blend’s current ingredients with the team if you follow a vegan diet.

How do I use and store my jar?+

Follow the serving and storage instructions supplied with your jar. The Super Seamoss team can help with guidance for your chosen blend.

Which blend is suitable for me?+

Check the ingredients and blend-specific guidance before ordering. The brand advises against Rich Clarification during pregnancy, and All Night Long during pregnancy or if you have heart problems. Ask a qualified health professional if you need advice about your circumstances.

Can I use these blends during pregnancy or breastfeeding?+

If you are pregnant or breastfeeding, check the full ingredients with your midwife, GP or pharmacist before using any blend. Avoid blends containing ashwagandha during pregnancy or breastfeeding.
