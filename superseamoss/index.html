BEFORE YOU ORDER

Know your jar.

How do I order?+

Message Super Seamoss on Instagram with the blend you’re interested in. The team can confirm current prices, availability and delivery before you pre-order.

What’s inside each blend?+

Open a blend above to see the ingredients featured in its original Instagram post. Ask the team for the full current ingredient and allergen information before ordering.

Are all the blends vegan?+

Manuka Glow contains manuka honey. Check your chosen blend’s current ingredients with the team if you follow a vegan diet.

How do I use and store my jar?+

Follow the serving and storage instructions supplied with your jar. The Super Seamoss team can help with guidance for your chosen blend.

Which blend is suitable for me?+

Check the ingredients and blend-specific guidance before ordering. The brand advises against Rich Clarification during pregnancy, and All Night Long during pregnancy or if you have heart problems. Ask a qualified health professional if you need advice about your circumstances.
