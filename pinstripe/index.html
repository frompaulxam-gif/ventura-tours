The investor offer.

“You fund this run’s actual supplier and import costs, capped at £1,250. Available sales cash pays your capital back first, after refunds and delivery costs.

Once all batch costs are covered, you receive 20% of this run’s net profit. No shares or rights to future drops. We agree costs, unsold stock and a settlement date in writing.”

Repayment depends on sales. There is no personal money-back guarantee. The estimated share at sell-out is £265 if imports are included, or £210 with the extra allowance.

Test the demand.

The footage is strong. The 500 popup contacts are a useful first audience, but sales are unproven and email/SMS may overlap.

Show the £64.95 price and ask for sizes before ordering. Use replies and popup sales to set the size split. Free reservations show interest; paid orders prove demand.

Gift no more than 10. Choose creators whose audience fits and agree the content first. Lend samples to any extra creators. Gifted promotional posts need a clear ad label.
