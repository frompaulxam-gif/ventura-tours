what happens to my notes?

the app saves in this browser or Home Screen app on this device. anyone can open the page, but the page does not upload your notes. this is not an encrypted vault.

Safari and the Home Screen app can keep separate storage. make a test note in the app you intend to use. export a backup before removing it or clearing website data.

voice recording keeps checkpoints while the page stays active. always stop and wait for the saved draft confirmation before leaving. if the phone suspends the page, recovered audio may be incomplete or unplayable. check playback before relying on it.

automatic sync, AI transcription, multiple choice clarification and turning a note into a task still need the private Jarvis connection. for now, you can copy a saved note into your AI chat.

one press to capture.

1. add to your Home Screen

open this page in Safari. tap Share → Add to Home Screen. choose Open as Web App if shown, then Add.

2. connect the Action button

in Shortcuts, create a shortcut called Capture note. add URL with the address below, then Open URLs.

https://venturasolutions.co.uk/notes/

then open Settings → Action Button → Shortcut → choose Capture note. press and hold the Action button to run it.

requires an iPhone with an Action button. Open URLs may launch Safari rather than the installed web app, so check where your test note saves. microphone recording needs a tap and permission. it does not start from the locked screen.
