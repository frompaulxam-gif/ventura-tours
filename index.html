Fair questions.

No technical knowledge needed.

What am I actually buying?+

A service to improve one agreed business process. That includes understanding the task, setting up the right tools, testing and showing your team how to use them. Any software subscriptions are explained separately.

How much does it cost?+

The first conversation is free. We then agree a price based on the process, the tools involved and the support you need. You see the scope and costs before committing to any paid work.

Do we need to change all our software?+

We start by looking at what you already use. Where suitable connections are available, we work with those tools. If you need something new, we explain why and what it costs first.

What if the AI gets something wrong?+

We agree where a person needs to check the result and what happens when information is missing. We test normal and awkward examples before handover. AI can make mistakes, so the checks are part of the setup.

Do I have to pay monthly?+

No. You can start with a standalone setup and handover. Ongoing support is optional and has its own agreed scope. Any third-party software may have separate recurring charges.
