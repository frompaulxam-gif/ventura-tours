A few things
you might ask.

Do we need to replace our existing tools?

We start with what you already use. We check what can connect reliably and where a simpler change would help. Any new tools and their running costs are agreed before the build.

What if we don’t know where to start?

Bring one task you repeat, a form that misses details or a process people keep chasing. The free first look helps us decide whether there is a useful first project.

Can we try an example before committing?

Yes. We can walk through a relevant demonstration using sample data. A demonstration shows the proposed experience; a working connection to your systems is scoped and tested separately.

How much does it cost?

The first look at one workflow is free. Wider audits, implementation and optional monthly support are quoted separately once we understand the work. We explain any software or usage costs before you commit.

Will you train and support our team?

Training and a clear handover form part of the agreed implementation. If you want ongoing help, we can agree a monthly arrangement with clear support hours, responsibilities and a review of what to improve next.

Will AI send messages or make decisions by itself?

We agree the approval steps with you. Important replies, decisions and spending can stay with your team. A pilot starts with a clear scope and checks before any live actions are enabled.
